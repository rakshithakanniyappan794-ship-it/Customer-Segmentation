# Customer Segmentation Analysis using Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on customer segmentation and analytics using Electric Vehicle (EV) market data. The objective is to identify different customer groups based on vehicle characteristics, analyze purchasing patterns, and generate targeted business insights through interactive Power BI dashboards.

The project was developed as part of a Data Analytics learning exercise to gain practical experience in customer analytics, segmentation techniques, and data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives

* Perform customer segmentation using Power BI.
* Analyze customer preferences and purchasing behavior.
* Identify key market trends and sales patterns.
* Generate targeted insights for business decision-making.
* Improve understanding of customer analytics and visualization techniques.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains information related to Electric Vehicles (EVs), including:

* Vehicle Brand
* Model
* Price (USD)
* Driving Range (Miles)
* Annual Sales Units
* Customer Ratings
* Market Segment

The dataset was obtained from Kaggle and imported into Power BI for analysis.

## Key Findings

1. Mid-Range vehicles represent the largest customer segment.
2. Premium vehicles receive higher customer ratings.
3. Mid-Range and Premium segments contribute significantly to annual sales.
4. Vehicle range strongly influences customer preferences.
5. Higher-priced vehicles generally offer longer driving ranges.
6. Customer segmentation helps identify purchasing behavior and market opportunities.

---

## Recommendations

* Focus on Mid-Range EVs to target the largest customer segment.
* Improve features in Budget EVs to enhance customer satisfaction.
* Invest in longer-range vehicles to meet customer expectations.
* Maintain premium quality standards to retain customer loyalty.
* Use segmentation insights for targeted marketing strategies.

## Conclusion

This project successfully applied customer analytics techniques to analyze Electric Vehicle market data using Power BI. Through segmentation based on price and vehicle range, different customer groups and their preferences were identified. The analysis generated valuable insights into sales performance, customer satisfaction, and market trends. The project enhanced understanding of customer segmentation, analytics, and data-driven decision-making.
